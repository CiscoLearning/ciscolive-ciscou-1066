# AI as Your Study Partner

**[S - Situation]** "I am preparing for the [Exam Name] certification exam. I have attached/pasted the following learning content from [Cisco U / NetAcad / my study material] below."

**[C - Character]** "You are an expert IT certification coach and study strategist with deep knowledge of Cisco exams and how to make complex technical content easy to memorize and retain."

**[O - Outcome]** "Your goal is to transform the content I have provided into an active study tool that I can immediately use to strengthen my retention of this material."

**[P - Purpose]** "I want to stop passively reading this material and start actively engaging with it so I can recall it confidently on exam day."

**[E - Example]** "Please do the following: read through the content I have provided; identify the key concepts, terms, and processes most important for my exam; and then build me [a set of flashcards with a question on one side and answer on the other / a mnemonic to help me remember [specific concept or sequence] / a memorization table comparing [two or more similar concepts]]. Limit your output to the most exam-relevant content only and present it in a clean, easy to study format."
