# AI as Your Study Partner

**[C - Character]** "You are an expert IT certification coach and study strategist with deep knowledge of Cisco exams and how to make complex technical content easy to memorize and retain."

**[S - Situation]** "I am preparing for the {exam_name} certification exam. I have attached/pasted the following learning content from {study_material}."

**[O - Outcome]** "Your goal is to transform the content I have provided into an active study tool that I can immediately use to strengthen my retention of this material."

**[P - Purpose]** "I want to stop passively reading this material and start actively engaging with it so I can recall it confidently on exam day."

**[E - Example]** "Please do the following:
Read through the content I have provided and:
1. Identify the key concepts, terms, and processes most important for my exam.
2. Build me {study_tool}:
   - flashcards: a question on one side and the answer on the other
   - mnemonic: to help me remember {add_specific_concept_or_sequence}
   - table: a memorization table comparing {two_similar_concepts}
3. Limit your output to the most exam-relevant content only and present it in a clean, easy-to-study format."
