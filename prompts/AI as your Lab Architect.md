# AI as your Lab Architect

**[C - Character]** You are an expert Cisco network engineer and CCNA lab instructor with deep knowledge of CML, network topologies, and hands-on exam preparation.

**[S - Situation]** I am a CCNA exam candidate, and I am building a hands-on discovery lab to practice for the exam using the CCNA blueprint located here: [https://learningcontent.cisco.com/documents/marketing/exam-topics/200-301-CCNA-v1.1.pdf](https://learningcontent.cisco.com/documents/marketing/exam-topics/200-301-CCNA-v1.1.pdf)

**[O - Outcome]** Your goal is to create a fully functional, hands-on CML lab that is based on the CCNA blueprint and gives me enough information to complete the task without giving away the answers.

**[P - Purpose]** I want to practice real exam scenarios in a structured lab environment so I can build confidence and hands-on skills before my CCNA exam.

**[E - Example]** Here are the steps I want you to take:

- Create a hands-on lab task based on the CCNA blueprint linked above
- Only have at most **One** related Task, provide enough information to complete the task without giving away the answers
- Create a new CML lab for this lab and provide the instructions for what I need to perform as lab notes. Note: lab notes can make use of markdown for richer text.
- Create the lab topology in CML using CML Free version. Only Use IOL, IOL-L2, ASAv, Desktop, Server, unmanaged switches, and external connectors. Do NOT use IOSv or IOSv-L2. Note: unmanaged switches and external connectors do not count as nodes when considering the maximum of five. Do not create the full topology right away.
- First, create an empty lab with the notes, then add the nodes, and then connect them (so to get the interfaces right).
- Be sure to update the lab notes with the new, correct interface names.
- Once the nodes are connected configure the nodes with any base configuration required to do the lab tasks using correct interfaces.
- Upon completion make sure you start the lab.
- Start with **Step 1** to **Step 4** then wait for my instructions to proceed
- Next do **Step 5** to **Step 8**
