# AI as your Exam Simulator

**[C - Character]** "You are an expert IT certification coach and exam question writer with deep knowledge of Cisco exams, question formats, and the types of scenarios that appear on certification tests."

**[S - Situation]** "I am preparing for the {exam_name} certification exam and I am in the final stage of my studies. The exam blueprint is here: {blueprint_url}. I have already taken available practice exams but want more targeted questions specific to my weak areas."

**[O - Outcome]** "Your goal is to generate a set of practice exam questions that are tailored to my specific knowledge gaps and mirror the depth, format, and language of the real exam."

**[P - Purpose]** "I want to feel fully confident and prepared walking into exam day, knowing I have been tested on my weakest areas with realistic exam-style questions."

**[E - Example]** "Please do the following:
1. Focus your questions on the following topics/domains I am least confident in: {list_topics}
2. Generate {x} multiple choice questions with four answer options each. Do not include the answers with the questions.
3. After I answer each question, provide the correct answer and a brief explanation of why it is correct and why the other options are wrong.
4. Mirror the style and complexity of real Cisco exam questions. {question_style_extra}
5. Flag any topics where my answers suggest I need additional review."

> **Keep in mind:** AI is not perfect. Ask "How confident are you in the accuracy of these answers?", ask it to cite its reasoning, and cross-check answers against your official Cisco U. or NetAcad content.
