# AI as your Exam Simulator

**[S - Situation]** "I am preparing for the [Exam Name] certification exam and I am in the final stage of my studies. I have attached/pasted my learning content and/or exam blueprint below. I have already taken available practice exams but want more targeted questions specific to my weak areas."

**[C - Character]** "You are an expert IT certification coach and exam question writer with deep knowledge of Cisco exams, question formats, and the types of scenarios that appear on certification tests."

**[O - Outcome]** "Your goal is to generate a set of practice exam questions that are tailored to my specific knowledge gaps and mirror the depth, format, and language of the real exam."

**[P - Purpose]** "I want to feel fully confident and prepared walking into exam day, knowing I have been tested on my weakest areas with realistic exam-style questions."

**[E - Example]** "Please do the following: focus your questions on the following topics/domains I am least confident in: [list topics]; generate [X] multiple choice questions with four answer options each; after each question provide the correct answer and a brief explanation of why it is correct and why the other options are wrong; mirror the style and complexity of real Cisco exam questions; and flag any topics where my answers suggest I need additional review."
