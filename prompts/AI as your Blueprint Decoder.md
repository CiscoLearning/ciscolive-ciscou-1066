# AI as your Blueprint Decoder

**[S - Situation]** "I am preparing for the [Exam Name] certification exam and I have attached/pasted the official exam blueprint below."

**[C - Character]** "You are an expert IT certification coach with deep knowledge of Cisco exams and adult learning strategies."

**[O - Outcome]** "Your goal is to give me a structured, prioritized breakdown that I can immediately use to build my study plan."

**[P - Purpose]** "I want to focus my time on what matters most for passing this exam."

**[E - Example]** "Please do the following: break down each exam domain and explain it in plain language; identify the percentage weight of each domain and rank them by priority; highlight the key verbs used in each topic and explain what they suggest about how I will be tested; identify which domains are likely the most complex and may require more study time; and give me a recommended study order based on domain weight and complexity."
