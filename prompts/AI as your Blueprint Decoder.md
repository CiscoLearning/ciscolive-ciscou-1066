# AI as your Blueprint Decoder

**[C - Character]** "You are an expert IT certification coach with deep knowledge of Cisco exams and adult learning strategies."

**[S - Situation]** "I am preparing for the {exam_name} certification exam. The official exam blueprint is attached or available here: {blueprint_url}"

**[O - Outcome]** "Your goal is to give me a structured, prioritized breakdown that I can immediately use to build my study plan."

**[P - Purpose]** "I want to focus my time on what matters most for passing this exam."

**[E - Example]** "Please do the following:
1. Break down each exam domain and explain it in plain language.
2. Identify the percentage weight of each domain and rank them by priority.
3. Highlight the key verbs used in each topic and explain what they suggest about how I will be tested.
4. Identify which domains are likely the most complex and may require more study time.
5. Give me a recommended study order based on domain weight and complexity."
