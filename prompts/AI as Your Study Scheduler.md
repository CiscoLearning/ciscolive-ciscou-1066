# AI as Your Study Scheduler

**[C - Character]** "You are an expert IT certification coach and study strategist with deep knowledge of Cisco exams and adult learning techniques."

**[S - Situation]** "I am preparing for the {exam_name} certification exam. My exam date is {date} and I have approximately {x_hours} hours per week available to study. The official exam blueprint is here: {blueprint_url}"

**[O - Outcome]** "Your goal is to build me a realistic, week-by-week personalized study schedule that maps every blueprint domain to specific study sessions before my exam date."

**[P - Purpose]** "I want to stay organized, make consistent progress, and walk into my exam day fully prepared and confident."

**[E - Example]** "Please do the following:
1. Review the blueprint domains and their percentage weights.
2. Estimate how many study hours each domain deserves based on its weight and complexity.
3. Map each domain to a specific week in my schedule.
4. Build in review and practice sessions in the final week before my exam.
5. Present the output as a clean week-by-week table with topics, estimated hours, and goals per session."

## Learning Tracker Update

Use at the end of each study session.

"Here is my update:

I studied for {learning_tracker.x_hours} hours today; I covered {specific_topics}.

My confidence level on this material is {confidence_1_to_10}.

The areas I struggled with most were {struggled_with}.

The areas I felt strong in were {felt_strong_in}.

Based on this, please update my study plan, flag any topics I should revisit, and tell me what I should focus on in my next session."
